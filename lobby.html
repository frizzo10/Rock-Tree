How a table works

  1. Sit down. Choose an open seat and name your club. You need a free account, just your email.
  2. Wait for the table to fill. Ten seats, ten managers. You can leave your seat any time before the league starts.
  3. Play the season. Draft leagues start with a live snake draft; salary-cap leagues have you build a roster under a cap. Then 27 games, trades, waivers and the playoffs.
