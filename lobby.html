Early access

Seats are real and fill in real time. When a table fills, everyone has 48 hours to build a roster, and then the 27-game season starts on its own at two game days a day. Not full after three days? With four or more managers seated, computer clubs fill the empty seats and it starts anyway.

How a table works

  1. Sit down. Choose an open seat and name your club. You need a free account, just your email.
  2. Wait for the table to fill. Ten seats, ten managers, and no draft to schedule. You can leave your seat any time before the season starts.
  3. Build your roster. When the table fills you have 48 hours to build a roster under the salary cap. Anyone who hasn't finished gets one built from their picks.
  4. Play the season. Play 27 games at two game days a day, yourself or simmed, and the server finishes any you miss. The top four go to the playoffs.

Tables start when they fill. Bring people you know.
