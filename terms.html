What Cooperstown Cash is, and what it is not

Cooperstown Cash is software for playing simulated baseball leagues that use historical player statistics and dice-based results. You draft or build rosters, set your lineup, and play a season against other managers and, where seats are empty, computer-run clubs.

Cooperstown Cash does not organize, conduct or take part in any contest for money. It does not set, offer, award or guarantee any prize. It does not accept bets or wagers, and it does not collect, hold, transmit or pay out anyone's money. It provides software and nothing more.

Free tables, private leagues, and computer clubs

Free public tables. A table seats ten managers. Your club name is visible to everyone at the table. When a table fills, everyone has 48 hours to build a roster. If you have not finished by then, we build one for you from whatever you had picked, and the season starts. A table that is not full after three days, with at least four managers seated, is filled out with computer-run clubs in the empty seats and moves on to the same roster step.

Private leagues. A private league is a group of people who know each other. The commissioner decides whom to invite and approves only people they personally know, and members join because they personally know others in the league. A league may not be advertised to the public, have its seats sold, or be run as a business. Commissioners and members agree to the League Agreement, and we may close any league that appears to be public or commercial.

Computer clubs and missed games. Empty seats are filled with clubs run by the computer, marked “CPU.” If you do not play a game before its deadline, the server plays it for you using your current settings. Once a game is played, its result is final.
