Someone who knows you has invited you to play baseball with them. Here's what you're joining.

  • Everything runs itself. The schedule, the dice, the standings and the playoffs. If a manager misses a game day, the site plays it for them.
  • The money stays out of our hands. You pick the buy-in and the split, every manager sees the same numbers, and Cooperstown Cash never touches the money.
  • Your managers have plenty to do. Salary-cap rosters, an AI scout, and a live board showing who is in the money.

Start your league

About money

Cooperstown Cash never touches, holds or takes a share of your league's money. If your league plays for dues, that's between you and your league. The only thing we may charge is a seat pass for the software: a set price paid by each member (the commissioner's seat is free), the same in every league whether or not it has a buy-in, and never taken from dues or prizes.

Ask for a seat

Sign in with your email to ask the commissioner for a seat. It takes about 20 seconds and there's no password.

You'll be asked to read and sign the league agreement before your request is sent.

Start a private league

You'll be the commissioner and take the first seat. You'll read and sign a commissioner agreement first. Then share your invite link with people you know.
