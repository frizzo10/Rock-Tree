Private leagues

Play with people you know. You're the commissioner: you invite your friends, and you approve everyone who joins. Each league has ten clubs and a salary cap.

About money

Cooperstown Cash doesn't collect, hold, take or pay out any money in private leagues. If your league plays for dues, that's a matter between you and your league.

You've been invited to a private league

Sign in with your email to ask the commissioner for a seat. It takes about 20 seconds and there's no password.
