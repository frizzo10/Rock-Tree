Private leagues

Play with people you know. You're the commissioner: you invite your friends, and you approve everyone who joins. Each league has ten clubs and a salary cap.

About money

Cooperstown Cash never touches, holds or takes a share of your league's money. If your league plays for dues, that's between you and your league. The only thing we may charge is a flat hosting fee, paid by the commissioner from their own funds, never by members and never from dues.
