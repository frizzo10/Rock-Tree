Mock draft

Practice drafting against the computer. Take turns picking from 1,400 all-time player-seasons, then get graded: a score, a letter grade and your chance of beating its team.

  • Five challenges, from a classic draft to a $100M payroll crunch
  • A scout who advises your picks, and a 60-second clock you can pause
  • Play the team it drafted, and win games to unlock the legends

Learn and play

Quick game

Play for fun, or learn strategy. Pick a great club, from the 1906 Cubs to the 2022 Dodgers, and take on another. It's the practice field: learn the game here before money is ever involved.

  • Roll the dice yourself and watch how each player's card decides the play
  • Every play shows its roll and the card it came from, so you can see what wins
  • Full box score and play-by-play
  • Free with an account

People you know

Private leagues

Start a league with people you know, or join one you've been invited to. You're the commissioner: you invite and approve every member.

  • Invite-only, and every member is approved by the commissioner
  • Ten clubs, a salary cap, a 27-game season and playoffs
  • If your league plays for dues, you handle them yourselves through a service like LeagueSafe. We never touch the money.
  • Or sit at a free public table with other managers
