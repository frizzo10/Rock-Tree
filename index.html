Your path to a league of your own

Three steps, and you can start at any of them. Practice is free, and every step teaches you something about how the game really plays.

  1. Front office1

    Build a team with the AI scout, then get graded Start here

    Practice drafting against the computer from 1,400 all-time player-seasons. Sign in free and the scout puts his advice on every pick in his own words: who to take now, who the computer is about to grab, and what your budget can afford. When the draft ends you get a report card: a score, a letter grade from A+ to F, and your chance of beating the computer's team over 1,000 simulated games, with the scout's verdict on what you did.

    • Five challenges, from a classic draft to a $100M payroll crunch
    • A scout who advises your picks, and a 60-second clock you can pause
    • A report card with your team's strength, position by position, and your odds in a best-of-7
    Start a mock draft Resume your mock draft See your report card
  2. Learn and play2

    Play it and see how it plays

    Play for fun, or learn strategy. Take the team you drafted, or any great club from the 1906 Cubs to the 2022 Dodgers, out on the field. It's the practice field: learn the game here before you start a league.

    • Roll the dice yourself and watch how each player's card decides the play
    • Every play shows its roll and the card it came from, so you can see what wins
    • Full box score and play-by-play. Win 3 games to unlock the legends.
    Play a quick gamePlay your drafted team
  3. People you know3

    Played for cash

    Play for cash with people you know

    This is the real thing: a league of real managers playing for a cash pot. Your league sets the dues and how the pot is paid out. You're the commissioner, and you invite and approve every member.

    • Real managers in every seat you fill. The computer only fills seats nobody takes.
    • Ten clubs, a salary cap, a 27-game season and playoffs.
    • Adults only: you must be 18 or older.

    How the money works. Leagues that play for money can collect dues through LeagueSafe, an independent service that holds the dues and pays out the winners. Cooperstown Cash never touches league money and takes no share of it.

    LeagueSafe says setup takes about five minutes. Have ready your entry fee, how you'll split the pot, and your members' details. Step-by-step instructions appear on your league's page here once you create a league.
