Play-by-play

Every at-bat is a duel. You and the computer each roll a 20-sided die, and the higher roll decides whose card the play comes from: the hitter's or the pitcher's.

Whoever wins the duel rolls two 10-sided dice for a number from 01 to 100. The computer finds that number on the card, plays it out and keeps score.

Cards are built from each player's real season, adjusted for the league he played in. Pick your club and press Play ball.
